Where it started

A friend of mine is a flight attendant. Her safety and emergency procedures manual runs to 1,226 pages. General chatbots failed her twice: the PDF was too big to upload, and when one did answer, there was no telling a sentence from the manual from a plausible one the model made up. For the brace commands on a ditching, sounding right is a failure.

How it works

Two searches run side by side and meet in the middle. The agent reads whole sections, then points at the lines it used. Only the manual writes the quote.

Instruments

40 real crew questions, each with the section it should land on. The check runs with every test, so a slip fails the build.
